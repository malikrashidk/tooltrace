# Privacy Policy for Tooltrace Browser Extension

**Effective Date: January 15, 2026**

Tooltrace ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Tooltrace Browser Extension.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Domain Activity**: The extension monitors the domains of open tabs to identify supported SaaS platforms (e.g., Slack, GitHub) and track your software usage.
- **Session Data**: We synchronize your Tooltrace web app session via cookies to provide a seamless "Connected" experience.
- **Credentials**: When you use the "Magic Fill" feature, we briefly access your encrypted credentials from the Tooltrace server to inject them into login forms. **We do not store passwords locally in plain text.**

## 2. Browser Permissions Justification
- **`scripting` & `tabs`**: Used solely to perform the "Magic Fill" credential injection. 
- **`cookies`**: Used to verify your session with the Tooltrace Hub.
- **`webNavigation`**: Used to detect when you visit a SaaS tool for usage tracking.

## 3. Data Protection
Your credentials remain encrypted at rest on our secure servers using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Data transmitted between the extension and our API is encrypted via HTTPS.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

## 5. Contact Us
For more information, please visit [https://app.tooltrace.io](https://app.tooltrace.io).
